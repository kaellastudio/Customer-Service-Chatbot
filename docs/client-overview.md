# A support assistant for your store

*A short overview for store owners. The technical detail is in the
[README](../README.md).*

## What it does

Your customers ask the same handful of questions every day: where is my
order, do you ship to my country, how long does delivery take, can I return
this, can I change my address. Each one is quick to answer. Together they
take hours a week, and they arrive at all hours.

The assistant answers the routine ones straight away, on your website chat
and, when you are ready, in your email and social inboxes. Anything it should
not handle on its own goes to your team, with the whole conversation
attached, so nobody has to ask the customer to repeat themselves.

## What it handles, and what it hands to you

| Customer asks | What happens |
|---|---|
| Do you ship to my country? How long is delivery? | Answered instantly from your FAQ, with a link to your shipping page |
| What's your returns policy? Which payments do you take? | Answered instantly from your FAQ |
| Where is my order? | Asks for the order number and email, checks your store, replies with the status and tracking link |
| My order arrived damaged | Apologises, tells them when your team will reply, and alerts your team |
| Can I change my delivery address? | Never changes it itself. It collects the request and passes it to your team to confirm |
| Anything your FAQ doesn't cover | Passed to your team. It does not guess |
| Emails | Writes a draft reply in your inbox. You read it and press send |

## Why it's safe to put in front of customers

- It only says what your FAQ says. You keep the FAQ in a simple spreadsheet
  and can change any answer yourself, so it never makes up a policy.
- It can't promise anything you wouldn't. Refunds, delivery dates and
  exceptions are always your team's call, and the wording it uses when it
  hands over is fixed.
- Order details stay private. It only shares an order's status when the
  order number and email both match, and it never sees addresses or payment
  details.
- If something breaks, the customer reaches a person, not silence.
- Nothing goes out from your email without you. Email replies stay as drafts
  until someone on your team sends them.

## How we roll it out

| Week | What happens | What we need from you |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Log every customer question for two weeks: what was asked, where, and the reply. Publish a proper shipping and delivery page if there isn't one | Access to your inboxes, or someone to keep the log |
| 2 | Write the FAQ from the real questions. Set up the website chat in test mode | Sign-off on the FAQ answers |
| 3 | Website chat goes live. We review every conversation daily and fill FAQ gaps | A person named to pick up handovers |
| 4 | Add email drafts, then social messages once chat is proven | Approval to connect email and socials |

Starting with the website rather than every channel at once means it's live
in a few weeks, on the channel where your buyers are, and proven before it
goes anywhere near your inbox.

## What you get

- The assistant, set up on your website with your brand name and colours
- A support spreadsheet with your FAQ, a log of every conversation, and a
  list of open handovers
- Alerts to your team for anything that needs a person
- Two weeks of tuning after launch, based on what your customers actually ask

## What it costs to run

You pay for the automation platform (n8n, from a small monthly fee, or
self-hosted) and the AI usage, which for a small store is typically a few
pounds or dollars a month, since only real customer questions reach it. Spam,
newsletters and auto-replies are filtered out before they cost anything.
